        Engineering & Model Architecture
        
Why BERT-LoRA Outperforms Conventional Heuristics

        
A peek under the hood of the training dynamics, parameter efficiency, and cryptographic anti-leakage audit.

      


      

        
        
        

          

            
          

          
Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)

          

            Rather than fine-tuning all 110M parameters of bert-base-uncased, we tuned rank decomposition matrices on the Query and Value attention projection layers with $r=8$ and $\alpha=16$.
          

          

            

              Active Trainable Footprint
              296,450 params (0.27%)
            

            

              

            

            
Frozen Base Architecture: 110,000,000 params

          

        


        
        

          

            
          

          
Counterfactual Data Augmentation (CDA)

          

            Real food databases are heavily skewed towards Halal ingredients (>75%). To prevent majority-class collapse, we generated 206,500 hard-negative synthetic variations injecting negation prefixes ("zero X", "non-X", "X-free") across forbidden triggers.
          

          

            206.5k CDA Hard Negatives
            0% Cryptographic Leakage
            0.98 Haram Recall
